IEEEXtreme 20.0 · C++

Clue Duel

Two players race to name the hidden person, weapon and location. My solution treats it as a search over a tiny world, and lets the opponent's own guesses leak information.

  1. Shrink the world. One hidden card per category, so the state is just (person, weapon, location).
  2. Ask three at once. Each "shown" reply removes exactly one candidate, whatever the opponent picks.
  3. Learn the bot. Its guesses hint at the answer; how much to trust them is learned during the game.
  4. Decide with DP. A small dynamic program over the race says when to stop asking and accuse.
